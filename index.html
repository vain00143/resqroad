How it works

From pothole to fixed, in four steps

Every report follows the same transparent workflow, so you always know where your report stands and who is working on it.

  1. 01

    Report

    Snap a photo, drop a pin on the exact spot, and describe the damage. It takes about a minute.

  2. 02

    Verify

    The Road Maintenance Office reviews your report, checks for duplicates, and confirms the damage.

  3. 03

    Repair

    A maintenance crew is assigned, fixes the road, and submits before-and-after photos.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    An administrator verifies the repair and closes the report. You can follow it all on the tracker.
