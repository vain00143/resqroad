    Tips for a great report

    • Zoom in and place the pin right on the damage, not just the street.
    • Take one wide photo for context and one close-up of the damage.
    • Choose High only when the damage is a danger to vehicles or people.
    • Grey dots on the map are already reported, so no need to report them again.

    What happens next

    1. Submitted — you get a report ID to track.
    2. Verified — the office confirms the damage.
    3. Repaired — a road crew fixes it.
    4. Resolved — the repair is verified.
